Organizers

Organizing committee

  • Ziwei (Sara) Gong

    Columbia University

    Ziwei is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Columbia University, advised by Julia Hirschberg. Her research connects natural language processing, multimodal learning, psychology, and computational models of human cognition and behavior.

  • Lin Ai

    Microsoft

    Lin is a Senior Applied Scientist in Microsoft IDEAS Research and received her Ph.D. in Computer Science from Columbia University. She studies human–AI collaboration, context-aware and user-aligned agents, user simulation, and the evaluation of interactive systems.

  • Bolei Ma

    LMU Munich / Munich Center for Machine Learning

    Bolei is a postdoctoral researcher at LMU Munich’s Social Data Science and AI Lab, an Adjunct Assistant Research Professor at the University of Maryland, and a Junior Member of the Munich Center for Machine Learning. His research spans human-centered NLP, computational social science, and simulation.

  • Siyu Liang

    Rice University

    Siyu is an Assistant Professor of Linguistics at Rice University. His research focuses on multilingual and under-resourced speech and language technologies, fairness, and human variation.

  • Victor Soares Bursztyn

    Adobe Research

    Victor is a Research Scientist at Adobe Research and received his Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from Northwestern University. His research focuses on natural language processing, user intent and preference modeling, pragmatics, and LLM-based systems for complex real-world environments.

  • Julia Hirschberg

    Columbia University

    Julia is the Percy K. and Vida L. W. Hudson Professor of Computer Science at Columbia University. Her research spans spoken dialogue, speech and language processing, and computational models of human communication. She is a founding ACL Fellow, a member of the National Academy of Engineering, and a recipient of ACL’s 2025 Dragomir Radev Distinguished Service Award.
