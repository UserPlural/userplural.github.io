Submissions follow the ACL/ARR requirements and ACL formatting guidelines.

Paper preparation

References, the required Limitations section, an optional Ethical Considerations section, and appendices do not count toward the content limit. Limitations and Ethical Considerations follow the conclusion and precede references; appendices follow references. Papers must be self-contained, as reviewers are not required to read appendices.

Follow the linked ACL/ARR policies on originality, authorship, concurrent submissions, research ethics, and AI assistance.

ARR commitments

Commitments must include completed ARR reviews and a meta-review and satisfy the current ARR commitment requirements. Papers must no longer be under ARR review. Authors submitting to ARR must also follow its current profile, reviewing-service, and submission policies.

See the call for papers for the research scope and important dates for updates. Questions: theuserplural@gmail.com.
